# Call conversion review — all eight designs

Reviewed November 7, 2026. These changes are conversion hypotheses, not evidence of increased calls. This is a catalog review; it does not personalize or publish an owner’s site.

## What the configuration supports

`src/agent.config.json` remains the single source for the name, role, market, biography, service areas, brokerage, license, phone, email, and address. The current identity is explicitly a demonstration. The market, areas, contact information, brokerage, and license are placeholders. No verified experience, special credentials, testimonials, sales results, awards, local market observations, or first-call service commitments were supplied.

The existing biography describes clear communication, preparation, and personal attention. Copy uses those themes and invites questions about buying and selling; it does not promise an appointment, availability, a response time, a free consultation, a valuation, or a particular outcome. Stock property imagery remains illustrative. The sample portrait stays labeled and is never used when demo mode is off and no owner photo is configured.

## Shared call and trust paths

- The section immediately below each hero presents the biography, portrait, configured service areas, brokerage/license wording, and a design-specific call invitation. Hero portrait/name cards are intentionally omitted. No additional experience or expertise is inferred.
- Desktop headers show a clear call label and readable phone number. At widths up to 1000px, a persistent call bar provides the name and number. Bottom page space, scroll padding, safe-area padding, and first-hero spacing keep it usable; rounded controls follow Neighborly and Solstice.
- Call links use the shared normalized `phoneHref`. Visitors can read or copy the formatted number on desktop. Primary buyer/seller/About calls now dial directly. Contact puts calling first and retains email as an alternative. About uses the same portrait component and sample labeling as the homepage.
- Navigation, skip link, semantic headings/landmarks, image descriptions, visible focus, motion controls, and reduced-motion handling remain in place. Native disclosure questions and phone controls have comfortable target sizes. Hero shading and selected muted colors were adjusted for readability.

## Owner content needed before launch

Provide this in one batch for the selected design; keep `demo: true` until reviewed:

1. Real public name, role, brand, market, service areas, business phone/email, office address, brokerage, and approved license/disclosure wording.
2. A licensed real headshot, an approved first-person introduction, and any verifiable experience or credentials the owner wants to publish. Check the actual photo crop at mobile and desktop sizes.
3. Specific buyer/seller services and communication practices. Confirm all design prose against those practices. Only add call length, fees, availability, callback expectations, or deliverables when explicitly approved and true.
4. Owner-approved local observations with sources and dates where needed. The current design uses configured area names without asserting local expertise or current market conditions.
5. Confirmed HTTPS domain, approved privacy/accessibility statements and advertising disclosures, approved imagery/social card, and review of sample editorial content.

Testimonials and performance claims are optional, and remain absent unless authentic, permitted, and verified. Do not fill gaps with stock endorsements or invented market statistics.

## Validation and practical limits

- Inspected all eight home designs in a browser at 1440×1000 and 390×844, including hero messaging, the agent introduction below the hero, and the mobile phone bar. Additional checks cover narrow layouts, interior-page call paths, keyboard access, and configurable identity behavior.
- Repository validation under Node 24: `npm test`, `npm run check`, `npm run build:catalog` (including the production build), `npm run verify:catalog`, and `npm run test:exports`. Catalog verification passed for 1,984 local links/assets plus all starter downloads, using a local preview origin for the build. The independent export suite overrides the configured phone and checks hero/header/mobile-bar destinations, interior phone links, no preview links, builds, and existing editorial/metadata safeguards.
- `npm run verify:launch` deliberately fails on the supplied demo identity and example URL. This is an unresolved owner launch requirement, not a reason to remove the guard or disable demo mode.
- Browser sizing is a desktop-browser viewport test, not testing on physical iOS/Android devices. The demo number cannot establish a real dialer handoff, call connection, answered call, or email delivery. No production deployment or real-owner headshot check was performed. Automated checks and visual inspection do not establish WCAG or advertising compliance.

## How to test the hypotheses

After personalization and launch, compare each selected site against its own baseline. Useful measures are phone-link activations by placement, mobile versus desktop activations, and qualified conversations reported by the agent. A click is not a completed call. Review the clarity and relevance of the questions callers bring. Use an owner-approved, privacy-reviewed measurement setup; none was added here. Test one material copy or placement change at a time before attributing any difference to the design.
