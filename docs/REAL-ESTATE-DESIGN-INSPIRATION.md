# Real estate website design inspiration

Research date: November 3, 2026.

**Owner selection, November 7:** Fantastic Frank, Living Room Realty, Aucoot, Puente Romano, Aaron Kirman, Forster Jones International, Tim Smith, and Adrian William. See [the implemented collection](DESIGN-COLLECTION.md) for their original design interpretations.

Original brief: Requested scope: find approximately 30 impressive real estate websites, then choose eight with the owner before designing replacements for this catalog.

Three Terra research agents searched official agent, team, and agency sites. All 30 candidates below received a rendered desktop homepage inspection across the research team and primary agent. Observations are first-impression design judgments, not a performance ranking or a full usability audit. Mobile layouts, keyboard behavior, accessibility, and all interior pages have not been systematically tested. Some initial captures showed entrance animations, delayed video loading, cookie banners, or signup overlays; later primary-agent checks resolved the obscured heroes for Tracy Tutor, Keri White, Noble Black, Tim Smith, and Cindy Raney.

Links point directly to the first-party sites that support each observation. Agency references are included deliberately for art direction that can translate to an individual agent. The suggested eight are provisional: the owner has not selected or approved them.

## Implications for this catalog

The most transferable ingredients are photographic art direction, deliberate image crops, strong type scale, a distinctive identity, and disciplined spacing. Video is useful for some directions but is not required for all eight. A future design should also remain convincing without listings or unverified sales claims. Use original composition, copy and licensed media; reference sites' logos, photography, illustrations and claims are not starter assets.

Next decision: review the suggested eight, record the owner's keep/pass choices, and compare alternatives where a direction does not appeal. Do not treat this research as approval to implement new templates.
