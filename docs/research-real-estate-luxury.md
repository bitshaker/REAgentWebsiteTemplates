# Luxury agent and team website inspiration

Research date: 2026-11-03. Ten first-party agent/team sites, selected for a cinematic luxury direction. These are design references, not endorsements or verified sales claims. All ten homepages received desktop visual inspection; observations are snapshots, sometimes taken during entrance animations. Motion, full pages, mobile, accessibility, and functionality were not systematically tested. Site content and listings change.

## Preliminary strongest three

1. **[HEIDER](https://heidercompany.com/)** — A cinematic opening with an explicit play control and clear buyer/seller routes. Strongest starting point for a film-led, usable agent template.
2. **[Joyce Rey](https://joycerey.com/)** — Architectural imagery, signature wordmark, and navigation along the lower edge of a rounded hero produce a distinctive, elegant composition.
3. **[Josh Flagg](https://joshflagg.com/)** — The illustrated streetscape and large editorial serif create a memorable personality-led counterpoint to the standard mansion-video aesthetic.

## Candidates

| Site | Observed differentiator and transferable idea | Tradeoff / review limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [HEIDER](https://heidercompany.com/) | **Visually inspected:** dark cinematic opening, centered geometric brand, buyer and seller links in the top navigation, and a prominent circular play control. Adapt the cinematic introduction while giving both audiences immediate routes. | Video needs licensed media, a poster and reduced-motion alternative; initial loading appeared before the hero. |
| [Joyce Rey](https://joycerey.com/) | **Visually inspected:** full-width architectural hero with signature at top, oversized translucent type and a distinctive bottom navigation inside a rounded lower corner. Adapt the signature and low-slung navigation as an editorial frame. | Hero text was faint in the captured animation; ensure readable contrast and obvious navigation at rest. |
| [Josh Flagg](https://joshflagg.com/) | **Visually inspected:** a hand-drawn storefront streetscape sits over a huge elegant serif introduction. The site also has separate sales, media and neighborhood sections. Adapt the illustrated local identity rather than relying entirely on mansion photos. | The illustrated intro and cookie overlay delayed access to the underlying content; make entry and key links immediate. |
| [Aaron Kirman](https://aaronkirman.com/) | **Visually inspected:** framed nighttime aerial estate image, restrained serif wordmark centered in navigation, thin gold rule and large editorial headline. Adapt the framed image and muted metallic accents for an architectural portfolio. | Strongly dependent on premium imagery; avoid faint text and offer a graceful still-image fallback. |
| [Forster Jones International](https://forsterjonesintl.com/) | **Visually inspected:** white outer frame, centered wordmark and a dramatically oversized monogram overlay on a twilight poolside hero. Adapt the framed monogram as a brand device. | Brand mark can compete with content; surface an accessible heading and clear action immediately. |
| [Jade Mills](https://jademillsestates.com/) | **Visually inspected:** dark city-at-night hero with an estate-side sculpture, restrained central copy and a single search action. Adapt the atmospheric city-to-home transition. | Captured text was low contrast during animation; verify final contrast and avoid unverifiable performance claims. |
| [Smith & Berg Property Group](https://smithandberg.com/) | **Visually inspected:** aerial property background framed by a white margin, centered all-caps team name and italic supporting line; its navigation also gives a branded lifestyle feature a place alongside properties. Adapt the typography pairing and local editorial feature. | This is a familiar real estate hero pattern; replace ranking assertions with verified, owner-supplied facts. |
| [The Altman Brothers](https://www.thealtmanbrothers.com/) | **Visually inspected:** gold team identity and direct centered call to action over a nighttime aerial estate. Adapt a high-contrast, concise listing entry point with a distinct wordmark. | Its look relies on unusually lavish property photography; visual impact may not transfer without suitable licensed imagery. |
| [The Jills Zeder Group](https://jillszeder.com/) | **Visually inspected:** Miami skyline and waterfront establish location immediately, while the palm-tree team mark and direct buyer/seller navigation reinforce place. Adapt a locally recognizable landscape and locally meaningful visual motif. | Captured headline was faint during the entrance transition; keep regional claims and awards sourced and current. |
| [Tracy Tutor Team](https://tracytutor.com/) | **Visually inspected:** seaside footage fills the hero behind a centered, stacked thin-serif team name; a signature anchors the navigation, and an outlined press-story panel sits at the bottom. Adapt the coastal film and framed editorial panel. | A newsletter modal and consent banner obscured the homepage in one visit. Avoid interrupting first-time visitors, and provide a still image and reduced-motion option. |

These pages provide direct evidence of their own content and presentation, not independent verification of their promotional claims. A final selection should inspect the current desktop and mobile experience, keyboard navigation, loading, and motion before translating any concept into an original design. Do not copy logos, images, text, or claims.
