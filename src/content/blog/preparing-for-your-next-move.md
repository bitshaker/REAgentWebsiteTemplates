A home search starts well before the first showing. A little preparation can make the process feel clearer and more manageable.

## Start with your everyday life

Write down what you need from a home: space, accessibility, commute, upkeep, and proximity to the places you visit. Separate essentials from preferences so you can compare homes consistently.

## Understand the full budget

Talk with a qualified lender about financing and ask about costs beyond the purchase price. Taxes, insurance, maintenance, and association fees can affect your budget. Actual costs vary by property and location.

## Build your question list

Bring your questions to an introductory conversation with your real estate agent. Ask how showings, offers, inspections, and communication will work. A clear process gives you a useful starting point.
